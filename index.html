About Me

My name is Ahmed Alhamwi. I'm almost 21 and a third-year ICT Software Development student at ROC Mondriaan, where I started in 2024. Right now I'm doing my internship (stage) at Worldstream B.V. Previously, I completed my MAVO diploma in 2022 and continued with HAVO-level coursework, including computer science. From a young age, I've been fascinated by how computer programs work, which naturally led me to pursue a career in software development.

I started with HTML, PHP and Symfony, and today I mostly build full-stack apps with Next.js, React and TypeScript on top of PostgreSQL, Prisma and Supabase. I also build local-first AI tools in Python, such as a multi-agent coding assistant that runs on Ollama.

I am passionate about building innovative software solutions and designing user-friendly interfaces that make a real impact. I'm open to internship opportunities and business inquiries, so feel free to get in touch.
