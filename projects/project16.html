Project 16: Bowling League Manager – The Strike Group

A web application for running a bowling league, built as a team for the Werkplek Leren project. League admins manage seasons, teams, players, weekly matches and lane assignments, enter scores and generate printable PDFs. Bowlers log in to their own read-only portal to see standings, results and personal statistics.

Key Features

  • Seasons, weeks, teams and players with team linking
  • Lane assignments per week
  • Score entry for three games per player, with handicap applied
  • Score validation (a game can't exceed 300, a perfect game)
  • Match points, bonus points and a handicap table
  • Statistics: individual results, highest scores, handicap list
  • PDF exports: competition, handicap, lane assignment, players, score form, week
  • Role-based routing: admins get the dashboard, bowlers get the “Mijn” portal
