Project 17: GameHub – NextGen Developers

A full-stack gaming hub built as a team for the Show Your Skills project. Users play built-in browser games, browse 400+ free-to-play games via the FreeToGame API, compete on leaderboards, and write, reply to and upvote reviews. Admins and staff manage everything from their own dashboards.

Key Features

  • Chess with Minimax AI (3 levels), castling, en passant and promotion
  • Minesweeper, UNO (2–4 players vs AI) and Klondike Solitaire
  • FreeToGame catalogue with genre and platform filters (cached proxy)
  • Leaderboards per game, sorted by score and time
  • Reviews with 1–5 stars, replies and toggleable upvotes
  • Bookmarks and optional public profiles
  • Roles: Admin, Medewerker, User, Gast with a permission matrix
  • Google OAuth + email/password login; tested with Jest + a written test report
