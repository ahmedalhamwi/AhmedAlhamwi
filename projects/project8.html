Project 8: Rise to Monarch – Chess Academy

A free chess web app: play against Stockfish or another person in real time, and learn through a structured, AI-coached lesson system — with Elo ratings, XP, streaks, badges and a leaderboard. Deployed on Vercel with Supabase for auth, Postgres and realtime.

Key Features

  • Play vs Stockfish 18 (WASM, runs in a Web Worker in the browser)
  • Online play between two people via Supabase Realtime
  • Lessons with an AI chess coach (Claude API, server-side only)
  • Puzzles — every position verified against chess.js before seeding
  • Elo rating, XP levels, daily streaks and 7 badges
  • Leaderboard and player profiles with avatars
  • Row-Level Security on every table; service-role key never reaches the browser
  • Premium dark design, keyboard-accessible menu, reduced-motion support
